# Payment logos

## Interface symbols

House, truck, zap, credit-card, package, list, receipt-text, circle-check and circle-x SVGs are stored unchanged in `lucide/`
from https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide/tree/main/icons .
Lucide license notices are included in `lucide/LICENSE`.
They are decorative CSS masks so their color follows the page theme.

PromptPay logo: downloaded unchanged from Omise's Payment Methods documentation.

- Page: https://docs.omise.co/payment-methods
- Asset: https://assets.omise.co/assets/payment-methods/promptpay-fbc32c2f89ce4052261f385e563c62dcd19e527e4c80b35eeff79a65203833c1.svg
- Local file: promptpay.svg

The logo remains the property of its respective owner. Its presence identifies
the payment method and does not imply merchant certification or live payment availability.
